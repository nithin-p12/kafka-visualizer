Kafka meanings & simulation assumptions

Colors identify partitions. Filled circles and blocks are leaders; outlines are followers. Groups are logical memberships, not servers. Ordering is per partition. Consumption never deletes records. A committed offset is the next offset to consume.

Simulation: synchronous replication, acks=all, min ISR=1, immediate failure detection and safe commits of completed work. Stopped brokers keep their logs. Retention is a record-count limit. Groups alone do not guarantee exactly-once business processing.

Click a broker or consumer for actions; click a partition for its log, assignments and offsets. View changes never change Kafka state.
